Why North Shore property owners choose GDE Tree Services Sydney

Tree work on the North Shore often means working around steep blocks, multi-storey homes and tight access. Our crew combines qualified arborists, experienced climbers and EWP (elevated work platform) operators to manage complex jobs safely.

We carry out detailed canopy lifts to let more light into homes and gardens, reduce weight in heavy crowns and improve overall tree structure. For palms, we offer regular cleaning, pruning and seed pod removal to keep driveways, pools and courtyards looking tidy.

Concerned about council approval?

Many North Shore councils have strict tree preservation rules. While we don’t lodge applications on your behalf, we can help you understand what information is usually required and provide written arborist reports or photos to support your application where needed.

How we manage large and complex tree removals

Large trees close to homes, retaining walls and shared boundaries need careful planning. Our team:

  • Assesses tree health, lean, weight distribution and nearby structures
  • Selects the best access method – EWP, crane or professional climbers
  • Uses rigging and lowering techniques to bring branches down in controlled sections
  • Runs high-capacity wood chippers to reduce bulk on-site and minimise disruption
  • Removes or neatly stacks timber, and grinds stumps where requested

We understand the expectations of North Shore homeowners and strata managers – turning up when we say we will, working safely and leaving the property clean and tidy.
