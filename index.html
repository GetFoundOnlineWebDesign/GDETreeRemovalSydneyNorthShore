Safe, efficient tree removal – start to finish

Every tree and every site in North Shore is different. Tight access, slopes, nearby homes and powerlines all affect how the job is completed. Our crew will:

  • Inspect the tree and surrounding structures for hazards
  • Plan the safest method – climbing, rigging or cranes where needed
  • Lower branches and timber in controlled sections
  • Chip branches and remove debris or stack timber on-site, as agreed
  • Grind the stump if you’d like the area ready for turf, paving or replanting
